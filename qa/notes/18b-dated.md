A note with dates in its properties (notes/calendar.ts). Check: in the Calendar, December 2026 has a dot on the 1st (its due date) and on the 24th, 25th and 26th (from its start to its end, the time read past); choosing any of those days lists this note, and choosing the 2nd doesn't. Then right-click it in the list → Icon and Color → Purple: its dots and its event in the week turn purple, whatever color qa/objects is given; No Color takes them back to qa/objects' color, or the accent.
