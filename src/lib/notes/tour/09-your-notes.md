# Your notes, your way

## Private

lines has no account and no server. Your notes live on your device, and only go where you send them.

- **Save to a folder:** the button at the bottom of the sidebar writes every note to a folder as a Markdown file and keeps it up to date (in Chrome and Edge).
- **Export:** Settings → Data saves everything as Markdown files in a .zip, pictures and files included.
- **PDF and PNG:** ⌘P shows the note laid out on pages, ready to print or save as a PDF, or to save as PNG images: the whole note as one, or one per page. Try it on this note.
- **Locked notes:** lock a note with a password from the lock button above it. Its text is encrypted, and only its title stays readable.

## In the apps

The Mac app (and the iPhone app, coming) adds:

- Sync through iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Proton Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, or your own S3 bucket
- End-to-end encryption for synced notes
- An app lock with a passphrase or Touch ID
- Floating notes that stay above your other windows

## Make it yours

Settings → Appearance has sixteen themes, each light and dark (High Contrast and two for color blindness among them), and Contrast → Increased darkens grey text in any of them. Settings → Text has four fonts, and a note width and spacing you can tune. Under General → Layout, put the sidebar and the note list on either side, open notes in tabs, or show an outline of the note's headings beside it. Settings → Editing → Links chooses what a click, ⌘-click and ⇧⌘-click do on links, tags and mentions. Right-click a note to pin it or give it an icon, and swipe a note in the list to pin or delete it. ⌘-click notes in the list (or ⇧-click, or ⌘A for all) to tag, pin or trash several at once.

Most of lines is plugins, as in a code editor: LaTeX notes, the math and diagrams, TeX for PDFs, the themes and the icons. Settings → Plugins turns off the ones you don't use, and nothing in your notes changes: a LaTeX note waits as plain text until LaTeX is on again.
