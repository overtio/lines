# Images and files

Paste an image, drop one onto a note, or type `/image`. It's kept with your notes and saved with them.

![A sunset over the lake](attachment:photo.png)

Click a picture to select it, then Delete removes it. Drag the round handle on its corner to make it smaller or larger; double-click the handle for its own size again.

Pictures from the web show too. Type `/unsplash` to search Unsplash's free photos, or `/giphy` for a GIF, or paste the link to a photo on Unsplash on an empty line:

![Snow covered mountain peak illuminated by sunset light](https://unsplash.com/photos/snow-covered-mountain-peak-illuminated-by-sunset-light-mevyYnC5xCo)

## Files

Any other file works the same way, or type `/file`. It shows as a card with its name and size; double-click it to open it.

[Budget.csv](attachment:budget.csv)

Hover a CSV file for a button that turns it into a spreadsheet in the note.

A 3D model (STL, OBJ, 3MF, glTF, or a STEP or IGES file from CAD) shows above its card, to turn around:

[Bracket.stl](attachment:bracket.stl)

A terminal recording you made with `asciinema rec` plays above its card, in your theme's colors. Paste the link to one on [asciinema.org](https://asciinema.org) on an empty line and it plays too. Click it to play; Space pauses, ← and → skip.

[terminal.cast](attachment:terminal.cast)
