# Welcome to lines

lines is a calm place for your thoughts: notes in **Markdown** that turn into formatted text as you write. Move your cursor onto any line to see its Markdown again.

:::i
This is the real app, running in your browser. Everything here works: write, edit, delete, try every feature. The notes in the list show what lines can do, one feature per note.
:::

## Where your notes go

Your notes are saved in this browser, on this computer, and nothing is sent anywhere: this website only delivers the app. Another browser or computer starts fresh.

:::!
Browsers don't keep a website's data forever. Clearing your browsing data deletes these notes, the browser may remove them when the disk is full, and Safari removes them after 7 days without a visit.
:::

To keep your notes, click **Save to a folder** at the bottom of the sidebar and pick a folder (in Chrome or Edge). lines writes every note there as a Markdown file, with pictures and files beside them, and keeps the folder up to date as you write. Or export everything as a .zip from Settings → Data.
