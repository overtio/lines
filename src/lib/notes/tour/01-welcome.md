# Welcome to lines

lines is a calm place for your thoughts: notes in **Markdown** that turn into formatted text as you write. Move your cursor onto any line to see its Markdown again.

:::i
This is the real app, running in your browser. Everything here works: write, edit, delete, try every feature. The notes in the list show what lines can do, one feature per note.
:::

## Where your notes go

Your notes stay in this browser, on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. To keep them as files, click **Save to a folder** at the bottom of the sidebar and pick a folder: lines writes every note there as a Markdown file and keeps it up to date as you write. You can also export everything as a .zip from Settings → Sync & Data.
