# Your notes, your way

## Private

lines has no account and no server. Your notes live on your device, and only go where you send them.

- **Save to a folder:** the button at the bottom of the sidebar writes every note to a folder as a Markdown file and keeps it up to date (in Chrome and Edge).
- **Export:** Settings → Export and Import saves everything in a .zip, each note as a file in its own format (Markdown, LaTeX or CSV), pictures and files included.
- **PDF and PNG:** ⌘P shows the note laid out on pages, ready to print or save as a PDF, or to save as PNG images: the whole note as one, or one per page. Try it on this note.
- **Locked notes:** lock a note with a password from the lock button above it. Its text is encrypted, and only its title stays readable.

## In the apps

The Mac app (and the iPhone app, coming) adds:

- Sync through iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Proton Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, or your own S3 bucket
- End-to-end encryption for synced notes
- An app lock with a passphrase or Touch ID
- Floating notes that stay above your other windows

## Make it yours

- **Look:** Settings → Presets changes the whole look at once, not just the colors: Classic is a desktop of the late 90s, Pixel is one-bit pixels, Neon glows, Puddle is a rainy day in a picture book, Lightbulb a spark of lightning, Adventure a pixel-art adventure game, Aero glossy 2000s glass, Brutalist thick rules and big type, Constructivist a 1920s poster in red and black, and Void takes away everything but the words. **Save as Preset** keeps your own look, theme, paper, cursor and all, to come back to or share as a file. Settings → Theme has twenty-six themes, each light and dark (High Contrast and two for color blindness among them), Contrast → Increased darkens grey text in any of them, and Dark panes keeps the note light beside a dark sidebar and note list. **New** makes a theme of your own from one to change color by color, down to each heading level, bold, italics and links, which you can share as a file, and **Change colors by right-clicking** lets you right-click anything to change its color where you see it, then save it as a theme; **Import** reads themes made for VS Code, Zed, Base16, iTerm2, Ghostty and other terminals. Settings → Plugins → Paper puts ruled lines, dots or squares behind your notes, a paper texture under them, and a backdrop behind the page: a gradient, a color or a picture of yours. `/page break` splits a note into pages over it, and starts a new page in its PDF. Settings → Typography has seven fonts (IBM Plex Sans, Fira Sans and Agave among them), and a note width and spacing to tune.
- **Cursor:** Settings → Cursor makes the text cursor a block, an underline or a thick bar, in any color, gliding or trailing as it moves, glowing or throwing sparks as you type; and the pointer 90s pixels or a dot, with sparkles behind it.
- **Company:** Settings → Plugins → Mascots brings a pixel cat, alien, slime or girl to walk about the note, sit at the end of the sentence you're writing, cheer you on and nap when you're away. Pick it up and drop it.
- **Sound:** Settings → Plugins → Keyboards plays a keyboard as you type: a creamy linear, a clicky one, Topre, a typewriter with its bell. Add a recorded one from any Mechvibes sound pack.
- **Layout:** under Appearance → Layout, put the sidebar and the note list on either side, open notes in tabs, or show an outline of the note's headings beside it.
- **Two notes at once:** ⌥-click a note in the list, or right-click it and choose Open to the Side, to read it beside the one you're writing.
- **Links:** Settings → Editor → Links chooses what a click, ⌘-click and ⇧⌘-click do on links, tags and mentions.
- **The list:** right-click a note to pin it or give it an icon, or swipe it to pin or delete it. ⌘-click notes (or ⇧-click, or ⌘A for all) to tag, pin or trash several at once.

Most of lines is plugins, as in a code editor: LaTeX notes, spreadsheets and their formulas, the math and diagrams, TeX for PDFs, the themes and the icons. Settings → Plugins turns off the ones you don't use, and nothing in your notes changes: a LaTeX note waits as plain text until LaTeX is on again.
