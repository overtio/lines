# Books, movies and music

Paste a link to a book, a comic, a movie, a video, a song, a podcast or an article into a new note, and it fills itself in: its title, its cover, who made it, when, and a few sentences of what it's about. Try it with a YouTube video, or a book on Open Library:

```
https://openlibrary.org/works/OL450063W
```

The next note, @[Frankenstein], is one, as a link to it becomes. It's in the sidebar's **Library**, under Books, with its cover beside it. Movies, shows, videos, music, podcasts, articles and papers each have a shelf there too.

- **Or search for it**, with the **+** beside Library in the sidebar: books in Open Library, movies and shows in Wikidata, music in MusicBrainz, podcasts in Apple's directory. **Blank** there starts one to fill in yourself.
- **A link in any note**: right-click it → **Add to Library**, and it becomes a mention of its own library note.
- **Your highlights from Readwise**: Settings → Library → **Readwise** brings your books, articles and podcasts over, each with its highlights as quotes, and what you saved in Reader.
- **Your bookmarks**, from any browser: Settings → Library → **Import Bookmarks…** makes each a resource, with its folders, the day you saved it and its tags. Right-click one → **Look Up Details** when you want it filled in.
- **Books you keep.** A book's PDF or EPUB can be kept with its note and read in its place, as @[The Yellow Wallpaper] is: drop the file on lines, or **Add a Book from a File…** under the **+**.
- **Where you've got to.** Every book starts *to read*. Right-click it on its shelf to mark it Reading or Read; movies are to watch, music to listen to.
- **One undo puts the link back**, if it isn't what you wanted. Changed while it's looked up, the note is left as you wrote it.
- **Mention it** anywhere: @[Frankenstein] was written by @[Frankenstein].author in @[Frankenstein].year. And `@type.book` on a line of its own is your reading list:
- **Feeds and podcasts.** The + on the Library's heading subscribes to a blog, a site or a podcast: its posts are listed at the top of the Library under RSS Feeds, Atom Feeds, JSON Feeds or Podcast Feeds (or all together under Feeds: Settings → Library), and open read-only, as a note is drawn; a Hacker News post has its discussion under it, threaded. Save to Library keeps one. (In the browser only some feeds can be read; the lines app reads them all.)
